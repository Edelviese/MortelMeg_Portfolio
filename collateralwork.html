Landing Page

Project Overview

The HQ Flyer is a promotional asset developed to drive international student enrollment through Fil-Global Immigration Services Corporation. The flyer simplifies complex immigration data into an engaging page and a digital copy—covering the benefits of student visas, core service offerings, membership pricing, and Fil-Global’s step-by-step assistance.

Roles/Tasks Assigned

  • Web Design — Conceptualized and designed the whole website version from scratch, utilizing auto-layout and aligning with the Fil-Global’s design concept.
  • Tablet version — Created the tablet version with the utilization of auto-layout from the Web Version
  • Mobile Iteration — Refined based on the UI/UX designer’s comments and the design’s auto-layout paddings, font sizes, and layout settings

Goals

The goal of this project are the following:

01

To design a high-converting, single-page landing page that inspires users to pursue international education through Fil-Global Immigration Services Corporation.

02

The design focuses on clearly showcasing their end-to-end support on their international journey, student and visa pathways, and alongside a transparent breakdown of membership pricing.

Design Process

The design process began with low-fidelity wireframing to establish the core layout, which directly informed the transition into high-fidelity designs. Throughout the iterative process, regular feedback from the Project Manager and Lead UI/UX Designer was integrated to refine color palette, enhance visual hierarchy, and ensure the final product met the goal of this short project.

Timeframe

1 Week

  • 2 days wireframing and direct high-fidelity wireframing
  • Following days are application of feedbacks from UI/UX designer and Project Manager

Tools

About Me

BIO

Hi, I’m Meg Mortel! A BS Information Technology Student(graduating) at PUP Manila. I am an aspiring UI/UX Designer focused on turning complex user interfaces and problems into clean and more accessible digital interfaces.

Currently, I am expanding my skills in user research, wireframing, user flows, high-fidelity prototyping, and mastering tools like Figma. I am fond of identifying user needs while maintaining a visually appealing design in both mobile and web applications and I am eager to bring and expand my knowledge in design to a collaborative team environment.

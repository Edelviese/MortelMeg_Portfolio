Web Redesign

Meeng Cat Sanctuary
Landing Page

Project Overview

The MEENG Cat Sanctuary Web Design is a personal project of mine way back 2024. Without prior knowledge in UI/UX fundamentals and standards, the design was only inspired in user interface designs in pinterest. The primary objective of this UI/UX Project is to design a digital landing page that connects the sanctuary to potential future MEENG cat owners/adopters. It focuses on providing a clear background about MEENG Cat Sanctuary, an overview about MEENG lovable Cats, and the processes and benefits of adopting in the sanctuary.

Problem

  • Lack of Organizational Clarity and Trust: Users struggle to understand MEENG Sanctuary's background, core mission, and credibility as a legitimate cat rescue organization.
  • Ambiguous Adoption Process A lack of clear information regarding adoption steps and benefits creates friction, leading to repetitive and time-consuming communication between the sanctuary and potential adopters.
  • Ineffective Cat Personality Profiles The current presentation and introduction of the cats fail to engage users or effectively highlight the animals' personalities to spark adoption interest.

Design Process

To address the issues within the original MEENG Cat Sanctuary website, I began analyzing the problems in the old design and proceeded with moodboarding and wireframing using a custom asset library I created. After finalizing the web version, I expanded the design to ensure a seamless, mobile-responsive experience.

Timeframe

2 Weeks

  • 1st Week — Problem Analysis and Wireframing
  • 2nd Week — High-Fidelity Web and Mobile Design

Typography and Color Palette

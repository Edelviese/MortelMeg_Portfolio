Mobile Application

VROOM Car Rental Inc.
Mobile App

Project Overview

VROOM is a luxury car rental service in the Middle East built for people who love fast, high-end cars. I’ve combined world-class supercars with an easy-to-use app, making it faster than ever to get behind the wheel of your dream ride.

The project originated from a professional UI/UX design challenge to either replicate or build upon a premium car rental prototype, with the core mission of optimizing the user flow to make booking an elite vehicle as fast, intuitive, and convenient as possible.

Problem

  • Complex Car Rental Process — Reorganisation of the booking details into a logical classification system: categorised information into Vehicle Info, Renter Info, Rental Period, and Application Review, ensuring users can confirm and submit their bookings seamlessly.
  • Security on Unlicensed or Underage Users — In the Renter Info stage, the renter must upload his/her driver's license card which will be reviewed by VROOM before confirming and scheduling for pickup their booking application.
  • Renters miss out on Vouchers — Repositioning of Vouchers at the top of the Home Page to ensure that user see their vouchers that they can show upon payment and pickup.

Goals

The goal of this project are the following:

01

To minimise form answering difficulty by organising the booking application journey into logical steps such as confirming the chosen vehicle, renter information and license verification, rental period, and application reviews.

02

To ensure that the user is able to book or rent a car seamlessly starting with the ability to check the important details, specification, and condition of their chosen car up to viewing and reviewing their booking applications.

Design Process

The design process of this project consists of creation of simple user flows with low-fidelity wireframes using my own created assets, creation of high-fidelity wireframes, and basic prototyping.

Timeframe

2 Weeks

  • 1st Week — Analysis and Creation of User Flows with Wireframing
  • 2nd Week — High-Fidelity Design and Prototyping

Typography and Color Palette

The Vroom logo blends the letter 'V' with a camel head to represent travel, paired with a sunset palette of Orange, Yellow, and Deep Red. The sunset symbolizes closure, perfectly mirroring the user's rental journey: renting a car for the day's adventure and safely returning it at dusk to complete the cycle.
